## Original 3D explainers

Original procedural Blender scenes: fusion, spatial expansion, temperature equalisation, hypothetical Big Rip. The Big Crunch illustration reverses the expansion sequence. Native 1920×1080 renders, temporally interpolated from 12.5 to 25 fps. These are conceptual models; no claim of accurate object scale is made.
