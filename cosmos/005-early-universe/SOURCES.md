# KOSMION — Early Universe: visual sources and attribution

The finished documentary uses authentic observations and archive footage, scientific spatial reconstructions, selected supporting stock, and original spatial illustrations. Visualizations are illustrations, not direct recordings of early cosmic events. Published native dimensions are retained below; a Full HD master may include selected 720p scientific archival material.

Original audio from all visual sources is excluded, including the separately licensed music in the CEERS flight. The supplied narration, text and alignment remain private. Only one complete narrated MP4 is the media deliverable.

Original atom and wave scenes illustrate simplified spatial models, not measured nuclear shapes, literal electron trajectories, or a direct observation of a travelling photon. Planck temperature colors are false-color measurements, not visible colors of the early universe. Old globular-cluster observations illustrate old stellar populations and are not asserted to be the specific stars in a lithium abundance measurement.
