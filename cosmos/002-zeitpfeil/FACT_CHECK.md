# Scientific checks for episode two

- Microscopic time reversal: many standard dynamical models are time reversible. Avoid the claim that all fundamental laws possess simple time reversal symmetry; weak-interaction time asymmetry has been observed. This is not the direct thermodynamic explanation for everyday irreversibility. Primary sources: https://www6.slac.stanford.edu/news/2012-12-14-babar-discovery-top-10-breakthrough-physics-2012 and https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.5832
- Entropy: the large-system second law refers to the full isolated system; local entropy reductions, open systems and microscopic fluctuations require appropriate qualifications. Counting macroscopic alternatives must not imply that every particular mixed coin sequence is individually more probable than every-heads. Source: https://preposterousuniverse.com/blog/2007/12/03/arrow-of-time-faq/
- Gravity: gas can collapse and form stars. Spatial clumping does not universally mean lower entropy. Radiation, environment and gravitational degrees of freedom matter. Reverse reconstruction of an earlier supernova is different from later formation of a new star from recycled material.
- Early universe: nearly uniform hot matter is compatible with a special low gravitational entropy state. Avoid treating the Big Bang as an observed absolute first instant, the cause of every time concept, or a lottery with a known probability distribution. The reason for the low-entropy past remains open. Cosmological observational source: https://science.nasa.gov/universe/stories/quick-reads/what-can-we-learn-from-the-universes-baby-picture/
- Boltzmann: respected professor who faced major scientific opposition. Do not invent a simple causal link from ridicule to suicide, or claim that no evidence for atomism arose before his death. Einstein's Brownian motion paper appeared in 1905; Boltzmann died in 1906. Source: https://geschichte.univie.ac.at/de/node/36321
- Memory: records and the thermodynamic arrow are closely related; do not present all memory physics or consciousness as completely explained by entropy, or imply that any act of recording must necessarily dissipate a fixed minimum heat. Source: https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/2ch3q-t8e94
- Future: heat death is conditional, not a confirmed finite date at which spacetime or time itself stops. Hawking radiation remains unobserved directly for astrophysical black holes. Contraction and relativistic clock differences do not automatically reverse the thermodynamic arrow.
