## Original spatial explanation scenes

Created for Kosmion using procedural Blender scenes: orbit, gas chamber, close particle view, coins, thermal reservoirs, clock, pendulum, and a board-game illustration. Native 1920×1080 scene images, temporally interpolated to 25 frames per second. Reversed clips are editorial thought experiments, not recordings of reversed natural events. Conceptual models do not claim literal object scale.
