# KOSMION · Quantum observation — visual sources and attribution

This German documentary uses original spatial explanatory models, scientific reconstructions, genuine archive and observations, and selected supporting stock. Spatial models are illustrations, not direct observations of quantum superpositions, photons, gravitons or horizons. Distances, colors and timing in the original models are illustrative. Original source sound and music are excluded.

The supplied narration, script and speech alignment remain private. The finished movie is delivered as one complete MP4. Some scientific and historical source material is natively 720p; the 1080p master does not imply additional native detail.

## Scientific and editorial distinctions

- Decoherence is distinct from selecting a single measurement result. The narration preserves this distinction.
- The observable-universe radius and cosmological event horizon are different quantities. The cosmological example concerns an idealized de Sitter model, not a measured universal collapse mechanism.
- Quantum field illustrations do not represent literal electron orbits or directly observed individual gravitons. Cold Atom Lab and optical-communications laboratory archive illustrates real apparatus and research practice, not a performed black-hole experiment.
- Water and orchestra stock appear only with the narrated analogies.
- The scale scenes illustrate the expressly hypothetical Sun-mass black-hole experiment, not actual Solar System geometry.
- Earth surface texture: NASA Blue Marble, distributed in NASA WorldWind. Source: https://github.com/NASAWorldWind/WorldWindJava/blob/master/src/images/BMNG_world.topo.bathy.200405.3.2048x1024.jpg . Download validated as JPEG and SHA256-verified before conversion to PNG and packing into the original scale scenes.
