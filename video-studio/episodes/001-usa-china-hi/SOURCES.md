## Documentary scope

U.S. government archive sources are public domain as recorded. Chinese reporting excerpts retain their original contextual editorial quotation basis; they are not described as public domain or a blanket stock license. Their dates and source marks are preserved. Depictions of training and source-year equipment are illustrative and do not establish army-wide 2026 issue.
