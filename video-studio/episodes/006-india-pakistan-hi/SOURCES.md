# India–Pakistan: Hindi adaptation sources

This is a new language edition of approved Ukrainian episode 006. See `SOURCES-original.md`, `inputs/original-manifest.json`, and `assets/manifest.json` for original URLs, authors, licences, equipment identification, source windows and editorial limits. The old episode and its exports are unchanged. Assets were recovered from the immutable CRC-checked original source ZIP, not newly presented as current conflict footage.

Documentary clips retain exercise/year/operator qualifiers in Hindi. French/American exercise views and aircraft-family models are illustrations of the specified type, not authenticated imagery of the May 2025 conflict or of a particular national variant. No invented kill count or new military claim is added. Original claim sources are preserved in `claims.json`; this adaptation is not a fresh independent verification of all spoken claims.

Photos: original attribution and licence in the manifest. Sokolrus and Yiyuanju are Latin display transliterations of the original authors, whose exact original names remain in metadata. No photo watermark removal was performed. Ordinary source credits remain in the corner.

3D aircraft: FlightGear licensed source geometry/textures and corresponding COPYING files are included in `assets/models`; actual native banks are 720 frames / 24 seconds. Family/illustration captions are retained. No crude replacement tank models are used. GPL source is supplied alongside the rendered work.

Maps: genuine elevation mesh, 20× vertical exaggeration for readability, regenerated with Hindi geographic labels and a smooth camera. Natural Earth / NASA-USGS-NOAA-derived terrain / Mapzen provenance and terms in `assets/maps/SOURCES.md`; geometry code and DEM source tiles are included. Disputed northern borders remain dashed. No strike paths, deployments or claims of sovereignty are invented. No provider watermark is baked into the map.

Narration: user-provided Hindi MP3, unchanged words and tempo. ASR files are timing aids only, not a replacement script. Its low-confidence technical phrases were reviewed and timing corrections are explicit in `alignment/anchor-review.json`. A few phrase onsets remain approximate within about one second; the 11 chapter cues have explicit word-timed marks.
