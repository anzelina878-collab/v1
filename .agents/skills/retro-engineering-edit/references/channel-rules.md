# Stage-specific channel rules

## Current video production rules

For script, assets, editing, packaging, and verification, read the [PASTWORKS rules](<../../../../Данные проекта/research/current_production_rules.md>). They refine the previous profile: verify real footage of key actions before final scene alignment; the first 20–30 seconds show the mystery and the first minute begins answering; editing follows overall view → component → action → result sequences. Check each shot's meaning, orientation within the mechanism, and text density. Long episodes retain 3–7-second shots; Shorts use their separate 2–3-second range and format="shorts" under the relevant skill. The document covers rhythm, sequential use of a source, endings, and viewing with sound. Use an original photograph only once per video, even after cropping or processing; search for new real visuals when coverage is missing, then only rarely create an archival-style illustration without a public “reconstruction” label; retain internal provenance. A 20–30-minute target does not permit padding through repetition.

When a specific person is mentioned, show that actual person synchronously; verify identity through sources. For repeated mentions, use another unique shot of that person rather than repeating a portrait. See current channel rules for detailed checks.

Corrections established for the latest episode also bind future requests: expressive real motion in the first 3 seconds; video occupies most of the explanation time for a working device; exact phrase correspondence; zero reused original photographs and zero repeated/overlapping video segments. Crop and processing do not disguise repeats. Find replacements for unavailable sources and continue independent work. Before final rendering, audit 100% of shots and save a passed audit with hashes; after assembly, check final files and separately state actual viewing/listening limitations. Permanent parameters are in settings.json; the detailed procedure is in the current rules above.
