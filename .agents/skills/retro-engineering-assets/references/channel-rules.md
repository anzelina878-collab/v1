# Stage-specific channel rules

## Visuals and covers

The visual track explains the mystery: overall view → relevant component → movement/flow/force → constraint → test or result. The main subject is a device in action. Asset priority: video → photo → generation. Search for every component and action with detailed queries specifying viewpoint, model, and era; inspect broad Google results, video, archives, creators' pages, and YouTube. Justify switching to photos or generation with search results. An image of another variant cannot replace the required one without explanation. Do not draw infographics, diagrams, charts, or explanatory text cards for the shared multilingual video; meaning comes from component operation and narration. Show authentic documents only when the story needs them. Do not burn language-specific titles into the master video. Calm photo movement supports narration; endless zooming into one overall view does not explain operation.

Visual profile: metal textures, period workshops and infrastructure, expressive component macros, and observable actions. Apply light archival video.color_treatment: muted saturation and soft warmth without heavy sepia, detail loss, artificial shake, or flicker. Pass the setting into timeline and native FFmpeg; retain parameters in this channel's settings.json. The permanent thumbnail style from 9 October 2026 is a cinematic photorealistic engineering scene without added text, following the [user reference](<../../../../Данные проекта/research/thumbnail_style_reference_20261009/reference.png>): a prominent recognizable object, accurate mechanism detail, spatial depth, expressive directional lighting, and clear scale. Three variants are distinct compositions and meaningful angles on one topic in this shared style; choose angles by story rather than fixed numbering. Titles remain in metadata; covers contain no captions, arrows, frames, duration badges, or YouTube interface. This replaces previous typographic styles 3/6/9. At phone size, a text-free image must show device purpose through environment, result, or a related object. Episode 4's five thumbnails are a separate request; future default remains three. The full [thumbnail rules](../../retro-engineering-thumbnails/references/styles.md) and settings.json thumbnails section define current presentation. Language-independent compositions may be reused between requested locales, with three finished files in each. Search and generation restrictions remain.

Titles sell a question or contradiction while keeping the object clear. Not every title starts with the model name. Title, thumbnail, and first 20 seconds promise one explanation. Descriptions give context and important sources without replacing the script with an encyclopedia summary. Localization preserves verified meaning; Ukrainian narration belongs in one video, with no additional tracks under current delivery=[uk].

Search broad Google results, then archives and original publications; verify usage terms separately. Generated visuals are illustrations with provenance in internal manifest/search_log, not evidence of tests or authentic patents. Under the user's instruction of 9 October 2026, do not add a public “reconstruction” label to video, narration, cover, or description. Explain principles with real mechanism footage; do not add original text infographics to the multilingual master. Do not promise perpetual motion, a world record, or “banned technology” without evidence.
