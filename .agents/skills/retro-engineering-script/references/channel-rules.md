# Stage-specific channel rules

## Topics and scripts

Concept: an engineering mystery. Each episode answers one intriguing question about a real machine, technology, or engineering challenge. Topics span infrastructure, image/sound recording, computing, domestic mechanisms, transport, communications, manufacturing, energy, medical devices, control, and measurement.

Delivery: a calm documentary narrator with curiosity and a sense of mystery. Oddity → question → problem → engineering solution → new problem → resolution → unexpected verified fact. Hook within the first 20 seconds: a specific contradiction or unusual detail that the episode explains. Introduce dates and biographies when needed to understand a choice. Roughly every 1–2 minutes, develop the question through a new constraint, test, or consequence; turns must follow sources.

For Человек без лица, the story and narration are supplied by the user. Their language, duration and content take precedence over inherited Ukrainian-only delivery and engineering-channel calibration. Do not translate or rewrite them without a request.

Voice calibration is stored in the channel settings' script section and фактическую длительность предоставленной записи. Direct Ukrainian episode 2: 23,689 characters including spaces, 3267 words, 1617.330612 seconds (26:57.33). Working Ukrainian pace: 878.818 characters/minute and 121.200 words/minute; approximately 22,000 characters for 25 minutes. Count only spoken Unicode text including spaces, punctuation, and line breaks. Allow roughly ±10% variation and refine with subsequent long episodes using the same voice and speed. The historical Russian episode 1 measurement is stored separately and is not Ukrainian calibration.

Topic selection maintains variety between episodes. The current catalog has 50 topics: ten fields with five each, including one aircraft and one refrigerator. This describes that catalog, not a permanent quota. Future selections should alternate industries, scales, and operating principles; adjacent topics should differ. Do not replace an aircraft imbalance with another object-group imbalance. An explicit thematic request may specify a single-industry series.

Primary sources: manuals, patents, drawings, test reports, contemporary technical publications, and manufacturer/museum archives. Verify model/variant, year, authorship, purpose, and units. Distinguish concept, prototype, production item, and claimed versus measured performance. A patent does not prove successful operation. Do not infer failure or cancellation causes from another technology's later success.

Save sources, pages, figures, and technical uncertainties in research_notes.md; record the causal chain and length plan in plan.md. Do not invent engineer dialogue or physically impossible properties. Preserve the supplied text and verify its correspondence to audible speech.
