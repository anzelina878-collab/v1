# Permanent PASTWORKS thumbnail style — 9 October 2026

The [user reference](<../../../../Данные проекта/research/thumbnail_style_reference_20261009/reference.png>) is a screenshot of three channel covers: Orion above Earth, an open VHS mechanism, and Hoover Dam construction. Transfer the images' visual language. Video captions, dates, rounded cards, and duration badges belong to YouTube's interface and must not appear in finished covers.

## Cover appearance

One coherent cinematic photorealistic scene without added text. The main engineering object occupies a substantial part of the frame, with a clear silhouette and readable component. The environment conveys scale, era, and context: space and Earth for space topics, a workbench for a mechanism, a canyon and construction site for a structure. Angle, diagonals, and depth lead attention to the subject.

Use expressive directional lighting: metallic highlights, warm lit surfaces, cool muted shadows, and natural atmospheric perspective. Color depends on the story; do not apply the same heavy teal-and-orange grade everywhere. Preserve steel, wire, stone, and component textures. Small details support recognition, but the main object and its unusual feature must read on a phone.

## Clear device purpose

Purpose must be clear in a small image without reading the title. Connect the device to a recognizable object, environment, or result. For episode 4, the user specifically requested a pacemaker/heart connection: [variants with a heart model and anatomical demonstration](<../../../../Результаты/Видео 004/uk/тамбнейлс-1.jpg>). Adapt the meaningful relationship to other topics rather than automatically copying hearts or medical scenes.

Build anatomical demonstrations from verified topology; do not confuse a pacemaker with an artificial heart, ICD, or Reveal. The device, lead count, and connection routes must agree with sources. Do not make a generated illustration appear to be an actual patient photograph or archival test. Retain internal provenance and separately justify permitted generation; do not add a public “reconstruction” label.

## Three variants

Default: three independent compositions per episode in the shared style. Additional variants require an explicit request; episode 4's five belong to that request. Possible angles: object and scale, close internal component, device in action or its result. Choose story-appropriate angles without rigid numbering. Each variant promises an answer from the same episode. Do not create three crops of one source or the same scene in different colors.

For a small device, environment and viewpoint convey scale; do not turn it into a giant machine or add fictional gears. If a person appears, use a verified image of the intended person with a meaningful scene connection. Verify construction and action against real sources: attractiveness does not justify impossible geometry or an unsupported test.

## Design restrictions

No added titles, numbers, component labels, arrows, badges, frames, text logos, or visible collage boundaries. Do not reserve space for future text. Authentic markings on a real device are allowed as a small part of the object, not as the main explanation. Do not present the object as a catalog cutout on empty white. Do not copy YouTube UI timestamps, titles, dates, or rounded edges.

Previously selected styles 3/6/9 and typography examples remain historical choices and do not govern new thumbnails. This permanent style applies to future Retro covers. Already delivered files change only upon a remake request.

## Search and quality control

The rule of 8 October 2026 remains: thorough Google searches for real photos/videos, verification of original publications and terms, and generation only as an individually justified last resort in search_log. Found materials may be combined into a natural coherent scene while preserving technical accuracy. The reference itself neither verifies device geometry nor automatically authorizes generation.

Before delivery, open every cover at full size and around 320×180. Check a prominent object, clear visual mystery, title/hook connection, technical accuracy, no unwanted captions, and three distinct compositions. Long thumbnails use 16:9; Shorts covers adapt the same clarity and lighting to 9:16 without text. Text-free images need no language-specific redraw, but save finished files in every requested language folder.
